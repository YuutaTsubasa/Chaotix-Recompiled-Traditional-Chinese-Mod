## Body (Source mode)
```html
<p>A <b>Traditional Chinese (zh-TW, Taiwan)</b> translation of <b>Knuckles' Chaotix</b> for <b>Knuckles' Chaotix Recompiled</b> (the native PC / Android / macOS / iOS / Linux port). It is a texture mod: no ROM patching, just drop it into the Mods folder.</p>

<h2>What's translated</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>94 redrawn 32X sprites:</b> HUD, zone title cards, results screen, special stage and bonus stage text, World Entrance signs, the title screen</li>
<li><b>Every menu:</b> title menu, OPTIONS, DATA LOAD (save slots, SAVEMODE and DELETE boxes), TRAINING, PLAY STYLE, PLAYER SELECT / COMBI SELECT, the SOUND TEST header</li>
<li>Menu banners stay Chinese while they slide in and out</li>
<li>Names follow SEGA's official Traditional Chinese where it exists (納克魯斯, 艾斯皮歐, 貝庫特, 查米, 金屬索尼克, 蛋頭博士, 卡歐提斯小隊)</li>
<li>Pixel text drawn in the game's own palettes; works in 4:3 and widescreen</li>
<li>Source, tools and specs: <a href="https://github.com/YuutaTsubasa/Chaotix-Recompiled-Traditional-Chinese-Mod">GitHub</a></li>
</ul>

<h2>Requirements</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="https://github.com/YuutaTsubasa/ChaotixRecompiled/releases">Knuckles' Chaotix Recompiled</a> <b>0.9.0 or later</b> (older versions show some menu text in English)</li>
<li>Your own Knuckles' Chaotix (Japan, USA) 32X ROM, which the game asks for. Not included.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How to install</h2>
<ol>
<li>Extract the zip.</li>
<li>Copy the <code>Mods</code> folder (it holds the pack <code>ChaotixZhTW</code>) into the game's user data folder (Windows: <code>%APPDATA%\ChaotixRecompiled\ChaotixRecompiled</code>). Other platforms are listed in <code>README_zhTW.txt</code>.</li>
<li>Start the game.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Kept in English</h2>
<p>The CHAOTIX logo, ©SEGA, Lv, 1P / 2P, A: / B: / C:, FM / PSG / PWM, the SOUND TEST song titles, the cast list on the title cards, and digits.</p>

<hr>

<h2>繁體中文說明</h2>
<p>《Knuckles' Chaotix Recompiled》的繁體中文化模組。遊戲中的英文文字重新繪製成繁體中文：HUD、關卡標題卡、結算畫面、特別關卡與獎勵關卡、世界入口的看板、標題畫面，以及所有選單（讀取存檔、練習模式、遊玩方式、選擇角色、設定）。角色名稱採用 SEGA 官方繁體中文譯名。這是貼圖模組，不需要修改 ROM。</p>

<li>解壓縮後，把 <code>Mods</code> 資料夾複製到遊戲的使用者資料夾（Windows：<code>%APPDATA%\ChaotixRecompiled\ChaotixRecompiled</code>，其他平台見 <code>README_zhTW.txt</code>）。</li>
```

## DevNotes
```html
<h2>How it was made</h2>
<p>This translation was made with heavy <b>AI assistance (Claude by Anthropic)</b>: the AI inventoried the game's text graphics, proposed the translations, wrote the tools that draw the Chinese text into the game's palettes, and added the menu-text mod features to Knuckles' Chaotix Recompiled. I directed the project, chose the names and terminology (SEGA's official Traditional Chinese where it exists), approved every term, and reviewed the results in rendered screenshots.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>32X sprites:</b> 94 frames replaced as single-frame PNGs in the game's own palettes.</li>
<li><b>Menus:</b> Mega Drive menu text replaced by tile sequence (<code>mdtext_</code>) and by sprite group (<code>mdsprite_</code>), features added to the game in 0.7.0 and 0.7.1.</li>
<li><b>Fonts:</b> Noto Sans TC Black and Glow Sans TC Condensed Heavy (no anti-aliasing), Fusion Pixel 8px for small text.</li>
<li><b>Tools:</b> Python and Pillow.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Known issues</h2>
<ul>
<li>The names of the hidden characters Bomb and Heavy, and the TRAINING zone names other than Isolated Island, were not seen in testing. They are built the same way as the names that were.</li>
<li>Some rarely seen texts (Amazing Arena NO CLEAR / CHALLENGE AGAIN, Techno Tower LOCK ON, a few results and special stage messages) were not seen in play.</li>
<li>Not yet tested on a phone.</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<p>本中文化大量使用 <b>AI 協助（Anthropic 的 Claude）</b>：AI 盤點遊戲中的文字圖、提出譯文、撰寫把中文字畫進遊戲調色盤的工具，並在 Knuckles' Chaotix Recompiled 中加入選單文字替換功能。我負責主導專案、決定角色與名詞譯名（有 SEGA 官方繁中譯名者優先採用）、核准所有譯名，並以截圖檢查成果。</p>
```
