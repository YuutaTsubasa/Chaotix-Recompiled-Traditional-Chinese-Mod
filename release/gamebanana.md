# GameBanana page draft

## Title

Traditional Chinese Translation (繁體中文化) 1.0.0

(alternative: Knuckles' Chaotix - Traditional Chinese / 納克魯斯 卡歐提斯 繁體中文化)

## Summary

**English.** A Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) translation of Knuckles' Chaotix
for Knuckles' Chaotix Recompiled. The game's English text is redrawn in
Chinese - the HUD, the zone title cards, the results, the special stage, the
World Entrance signs, the title screen and every menu (DATA LOAD, TRAINING,
PLAY STYLE, PLAYER SELECT, OPTIONS) - using SEGA's official Traditional
Chinese names where they exist (納克魯斯, 艾斯皮歐, 貝庫特, 查米...). It is a
texture mod: drop it into the Mods folder, no ROM patching.

**中文。** 《Knuckles' Chaotix Recompiled》的繁體中文化模組。遊戲中的英文文字全部重新繪製成繁體中文：HUD、關卡標題卡、結算畫面、特別關卡、世界入口的看板、標題畫面，以及所有選單（讀取存檔、練習模式、遊玩方式、選擇角色、設定）。角色與名詞採用 SEGA 官方繁體中文譯名（納克魯斯、艾斯皮歐、貝庫特、查米……）。這是貼圖模組，放進 Mods 資料夾即可，不需要修改 ROM。

## Features

- 94 redrawn 32X sprites (HUD, title cards, results, special / bonus stage,
  signs, title screen)
- All menus in Chinese: title menu, OPTIONS, DATA LOAD (save slots, SAVEMODE
  and DELETE boxes), TRAINING, PLAY STYLE, PLAYER SELECT / COMBI SELECT,
  SOUND TEST header
- Sliding menu banners stay Chinese the whole way
- Official SEGA Traditional Chinese names; pixel-art text drawn in the
  game's own palettes (Noto Sans TC, Glow Sans TC, Fusion Pixel 8px)
- Works in 4:3 and widescreen (the game keeps its menus 4:3)

## Requirements

- Knuckles' Chaotix Recompiled **0.7.1 or later**
- Your own Knuckles' Chaotix (32X) ROM (not included)

## Install

1. Download and unzip `ChaotixRecompiled-zhTW-1.0.0.zip`.
2. Copy the `Mods` folder into the game's user data folder:
   - Windows: `%APPDATA%\ChaotixRecompiled\ChaotixRecompiled`
   - Linux: `~/.local/share/ChaotixRecompiled/ChaotixRecompiled`
   - macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/ChaotixRecompiled/ChaotixRecompiled`
   - Android: copy the `Textures` folder into `ChaotixRecompiled/Mods` in
     the phone's storage (after OPTIONS > EXTRAS > MODS FOLDER), or into
     `Android/data/org.chaotixrecompiled/files/Mods`
   - iOS: the app's Documents (Files app > On My iPhone > Chaotix)
3. Start the game.
