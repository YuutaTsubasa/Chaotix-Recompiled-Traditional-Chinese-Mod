## 1.0.0 (2026-10-23)

Initial release. Needs Knuckles' Chaotix Recompiled 0.7.1 or later.

- 94 replacement 32X sprites: HUD, zone title cards, results, PAUSE, goal
  sign, special and bonus stage messages, World Entrance signs and
  attraction board, blocks, speech bubbles, title screen lines and
  PUSH START.
- 18 Mega Drive plane text patterns: title menu, OPTIONS, DATA LOAD save
  slots.
- 31 Mega Drive sprite text patterns: the menu banners (DATA LOAD, PLAY,
  DELETE, TRAINING, EXIT, PLAY STYLE, PLAYER SELECT, sliding included),
  SAVEMODE and DELETE confirmation boxes, CPU, TRAINING zone names, PLAYER
  SELECT / COMBI SELECT and the character names, SOUND TEST header and S.E.
- Kept in English: logo, (c)SEGA, Lv, 1P/2P, A:/B:/C:, FM/PSG/PWM, the SOUND
  TEST song titles, the title cards' cast list, digits.
